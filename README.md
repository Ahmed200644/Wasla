📎 **Project Name:**    
# Masar-> 
> **University Admission & Coordination Experience Redesign**
>
> 📌 **Project Overview:**
> A UX/UI project focused on redesigning the university coordination and admission experience in Egypt. The project addresses the fragmented and confusing journey students face when searching for universities, comparing available options, understanding admission requirements, and tracking their applications.
>
> The proposed solution brings these stages into one clear and structured digital experience, helping students make informed decisions and complete their admission journey with less confusion and uncertainty.

> 🎯 **Project Objectives:**
> • Identify the main pain points in the current university admission journey.
> • Understand students' needs, behaviors, and decision-making criteria.
> • Redesign the coordination and admission experience around real user needs.
> • Make university information easier to discover, compare, and understand.
> • Simplify application steps and improve application status visibility.
> • Create a scalable and professional UX/UI solution based on research and usability principles.
>
> 📦 **Project Scope:**
> **Research:** User research, surveys, interviews, pain-point identification, and competitor analysis.
>
> **UX:** User personas, user journey, information architecture, task flows, wireframes, and usability considerations.
>
> **UI:** Visual direction, design system, responsive layouts, high-fidelity screens, and interactive prototype.
>
> **Core Experience:**
> • Discover universities and programs
> • Compare university options
> • View admission requirements
> • Understand minimum scores, costs, and available majors
> • Start and complete an application
> • Track application status
> • Receive important deadlines and updates
>
> 📅 **Project Plan (5 Weeks):**
>
> **Week 1 | Discover 🔎**
> Problem definition, secondary research, user research, surveys, interviews, and competitor analysis.
>
> **Week 2 | Define 🧩**
> Research analysis, personas, user journey, pain points, problem statement, value proposition, and information architecture.
>
> **Week 3 | Design ✏️**
> User flows, wireframes, interaction structure, content hierarchy, and initial design direction.
>
> **Week 4 | Build 🎨**
> High-fidelity UI, design system, responsive screens, interactions, and prototype development.
>
> **Week 5 | Validate & Present 🚀**
> Usability testing, design iterations, final prototype, case-study documentation, pitch deck, and final presentation.
