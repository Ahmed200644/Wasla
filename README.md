# Wasla | وصلة

📎 **Project Name:**
Wasla (وصلة) -> Guided Digital Presence Platform for Egypt's Micro-Businesses

📌 **Project Overview:**
A UX/UI project focused on helping small business owners, artisans, and home-based service providers in Egypt build an organized digital presence. Many of them run their work through social media and WhatsApp, which leaves their work scattered: photos in the phone gallery, orders lost in chats, and prices kept in memory. Customers, in turn, struggle to find and trust the right provider.

Wasla is a guided, mobile-first, Arabic-first platform that connects two users: the business owner, who showcases their work and organizes orders and customers, and the customer, who discovers trusted providers and contacts them easily. It is not a store and not an agency; it organizes how small businesses already work instead of changing it.

🎯 **Project Objectives:**
- Identify the real problems small businesses face in organizing their work and presenting it online.
- Understand the needs, behaviors, and trust criteria of both users: business owners and customers.
- Design a guided experience that needs no technical skills and works on mobile first.
- Make it easy for customers to discover providers, view their work, and get in touch.
- Define a clear role for AI where it reduces user effort (for example, writing descriptions and suggesting the next step).
- Build a complete, professional UX/UI case study backed by research and usability testing.

📦 **Project Scope:**
**Brand:** Name, brand personality, tone of voice, color palette, logo, mini brand guideline, and 3 social media posters.
**Research:** Stakeholders research, competitor analysis, user interviews and surveys, affinity diagram, empathy map, personas, and user journey.
**UX:** Problem statement, pain points, limitations, feature ideation and prioritization (impact-effort), value proposition, user flows, sitemap, and wireframes.
**UI:** Design system, high-fidelity screens for each user, and a fully interactive prototype.
**Landing Page:** A professional page explaining the problem, solution, features, value, and a clear call to action.
**Testing:** Heuristic evaluation, usability testing with real users, and a before/after comparison of design changes.

**Core Experience:**
*Business owner*
- Guided onboarding with ready-made templates
- Business page and portfolio
- Order and customer management
- WhatsApp-based ordering
- "Next step" guidance and AI-assisted content

*Customer*
- Discover trusted providers
- Browse their work
- Contact them through WhatsApp or an order form

**Out of Scope:** Payments, shipping, paid advertising, and agency services.

🧪 **Assumptions to Validate Through Research:**
1. The business owner's main problem is organization and visibility, not marketing alone.
2. WhatsApp is the primary ordering channel.
3. Customers are willing to use a platform to discover providers.
4. The absence of payments will not weaken the product.

📅 **Project Plan (5 Weeks):**
**Week 1 | Brand & Discover 🔎**
Brand foundation, name check, moodboard, stakeholders research, competitor analysis, and preparing interviews and surveys.

**Week 2 | Empathize & Define 🧩**
Interviews and surveys, affinity diagram, empathy map, personas, user journey, pain points, problem statement, and limitations. Finalize the logo, mini brand guideline, and 3 posters.

**Week 3 | Ideate & Design ✏️**
Feature ideation and prioritization, value proposition, feature sketches, user flows, sitemap, and mid-fidelity wireframes.

**Week 4 | Build 🎨**
Design system, high-fidelity UI for both users, landing page, and interactive prototype.

**Week 5 | Validate & Present 🚀**
Heuristic evaluation, usability testing, design iterations, final prototype, case-study documentation, pitch deck, and final presentation.
